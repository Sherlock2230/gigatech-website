Grid connection // the intake

Getting power onto your site — and enough of it.

A new building, a bigger machine, a second shift — every one of them starts with the supply. We prepare the installation, the paperwork and the meter position, and manage the process with ZETDC through to energisation, so you're not left chasing the utility between contractors.

  • New supply connections for commercial and industrial sites
  • Single-phase to three-phase upgrades
  • Capacity increases and maximum-demand assessment
  • Service cables, meter positions and isolators
  • Changeover and ATS for generators and backup systems
You bringSite location, planned loads, existing account (if any)
We deliverLoad assessment, installation, utility liaison
OutcomeA supply sized for what you'll actually run
MSB
Switchgear & protection // the main board

The board that decides what trips — and what doesn't.

Most electrical failures we're called to trace back to the main board: undersized breakers, no discrimination, poor earthing. We design and build switchboards with protection that's coordinated, not guessed — so a fault on one circuit takes out that circuit, not the whole building.

  • Main and sub-distribution boards, built and labelled
  • MCCB / ACB selection and protection coordination
  • Earthing systems and lightning protection
  • Surge protection for sensitive equipment
DesignSingle-line diagrams & cable schedules
ProtectionFault levels & discrimination checked
LabellingEvery circuit traceable on paper

MCC
Industrial power // the plant

Three-phase power for machines that can't stop.

Pumps, compressors, mills, conveyors and processing lines. This is where our mechatronics background matters: we don't just run a cable to a motor — we size the supply, select the starter, program the drive and protect the machine. Less tripping on start-up, lower energy bills, longer motor life.

  • Motor circuits — DOL, star-delta and soft starters
  • VFD installation, programming and commissioning
  • Motor control centre (MCC) and control panels
  • Power factor correction to cut demand charges
  • Industrial cabling, trunking and cable trays
AgricultureBoreholes, irrigation pumps, dairies, cold chain
MiningCompressors, pumps, workshops, plant
ManufacturingMills, conveyors, processing lines

Test, fault-find, maintain // the proof

Tested before handover. Looked after after it.

Every installation is tested before it's energised, and the results are written down and handed to you. When something does go wrong on an existing system — ours or someone else's — we find the fault methodically instead of replacing parts until it stops.

  • Insulation resistance, continuity and earth tests
  • RCD and protection-device testing
  • Fault-finding on existing installations
  • Scheduled preventive maintenance contracts
